TeX Gyre Pagella 2.501 web-font packaging

Source: https://mirrors.ctan.org/fonts/tex-gyre.zip
Upstream: https://ctan.org/pkg/tex-gyre-pagella

The four original OpenType faces were repackaged losslessly as WOFF2 using
fontTools and Brotli. No glyphs were removed or redesigned; original font
names, copyright records, and font metrics are retained. These WOFF2 files
are web packaging for this website, not an official upstream release.
The upstream license, README, and manifest are included alongside the fonts.

Web files:
texgyrepagella-regular.woff2
texgyrepagella-italic.woff2
texgyrepagella-bold.woff2
texgyrepagella-bolditalic.woff2
